# Lift maintenance agreement — Hydra Elevators N.V.

This Agreement is made on 2 March 2026 between Hydra Elevators N.V. (the "Contractor") and Marlow Court Residences Ltd (the "Owner").

## 1. Equipment

1.1 The Contractor shall maintain the four passenger lifts at Marlow Court, Rotterdam, listed in Schedule 1 (the "Equipment").

## 2. Maintenance

2.1 The Contractor shall carry out a preventive maintenance visit each month and the statutory inspections required by law.

## 3. Breakdowns

3.1 The Owner may report breakdowns to the Contractor's call centre 24 hours a day.

3.2 The Contractor will use reasonable endeavours to attend breakdowns within 24 hours.

## 5. Fee

5.1 The Owner shall pay a maintenance fee of EUR 14,400 a year, invoiced quarterly in advance (the "Maintenance Fee").

## 6. Parts

6.1 The Maintenance Fee covers labour only. Parts shall be charged at the Contractor's list prices.

## 8. Term

8.1 This Agreement runs for three years and then renews for one-year periods unless either party gives three months' notice.

## 10. Governing law

10.1 This Agreement is governed by Dutch law.
