# Agency agreement — Lumen Sports Management Ltd

This Agreement is made on 6 January 2026 between Lumen Sports Management Ltd (the "Agent") and Saoirse Delaney, professional tennis player (the "Client").

## 2. Appointment

2.1 The Agent shall represent the Client and use its best efforts to obtain sponsorship, endorsement, media and appearance opportunities for the Client.

2.3 The Client appoints the Agent as its exclusive representative for all commercial, media and sporting activities worldwide.

## 3. Duties of the Agent

3.1 The Agent shall consult the Client before negotiating any contract and shall not sign any contract on the Client's behalf without the Client's written approval.

## 4. Commission

4.1 The Agent shall be entitled to commission on all contracts signed during the term and on any renewal or extension of them, whenever signed.

4.2 The commission is 20% of the gross amounts received by the Client under those contracts, payable within 30 days of receipt.

## 6. Expenses

6.1 The Agent bears its own expenses, except travel at the Client's request, which the Client reimburses at cost.

## 10. Term and termination

10.1 This Agreement starts on the date above.

10.2 This Agreement shall continue for five years and may not be terminated before then except for material breach.

## 13. Governing law

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
