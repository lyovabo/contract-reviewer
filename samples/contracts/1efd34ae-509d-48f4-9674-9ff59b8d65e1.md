# Consulting agreement — Brightline Advisory Ltd

This Agreement is made on 25 August 2026 between Brightline Advisory Ltd (the "Consultant") and Sefton Water Services Ltd (the "Company").

## 1. Services

1.1 The Consultant shall carry out the review of the Company's procurement process described in the Statement of Work in Schedule 1 (the "Services").

1.2 Any change to the Services must be agreed in writing by both parties, including its effect on fees and timing.

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement runs from 1 September 2026 until the Services are completed, expected by 28 February 2027.

## 3. Fees and expenses

3.1 The Company shall pay the fixed fee of £96,000, invoiced in six equal monthly instalments.

3.2 The Company shall pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt. Reasonable travel expenses approved in advance are reimbursed at cost.

## 4. Standard of work

4.1 The Consultant shall perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, using the personnel named in Schedule 1, who may be replaced only with the Company's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

## 5. Intellectual property

5.1 Each party keeps the intellectual property it owned before this Agreement.

5.2 The Company owns the reports and other deliverables produced for it under this Agreement, once paid for. The Consultant grants the Company a perpetual licence to use any of the Consultant's pre-existing materials included in them.

## 6. Confidentiality and data

6.1 Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret, use it only for this Agreement, and return or destroy it on request when the Agreement ends.

6.2 Each party shall comply with data protection law. The Consultant shall process personal data only on the Company's written instructions.

## 7. Liability

7.1 Each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the total fees payable under it.

7.2 Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for breach of clause 6.

7.3 Neither party is liable for loss of profits or for indirect or consequential loss.

## 8. Insurance

8.1 The Consultant shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of at least £2,000,000 for each claim during the Agreement and for three years after.

## 9. Termination

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice. The Company shall pay for Services performed up to the termination date.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within 14 days of notice.

## 10. Independent contractor

10.1 The Consultant is an independent contractor and is responsible for the tax affairs of its personnel.

## 12. Governing law and disputes

12.1 The parties shall first try to settle any dispute through negotiation between senior managers.

12.2 This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction.
