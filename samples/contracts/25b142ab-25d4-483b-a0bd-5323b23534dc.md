# Data processing agreement — Pulsewave Health Ltd

This Agreement is made on 21 September 2026 between Ashby Clinics Group Ltd (the "Controller") and Pulsewave Health Ltd (the "Processor"). It forms part of the services agreement between them for the Pulsewave remote patient monitoring platform (the "Services Agreement").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Personal Data", "Personal Data Breach" and "processing" have the meanings given in the UK GDPR. "Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by the Processor to process Personal Data.

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Processor processes the Personal Data described in Annex 1, including health data of the Controller's patients, to provide the services under the Services Agreement.

## 3. Instructions

3.1 The Processor shall process Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the Controller, unless required to do otherwise by law.

## 4. Use of data

4.1 The Processor shall not sell Personal Data or use it for advertising.

4.2 The Processor may use Personal Data in anonymised or aggregated form to improve its products and services.

## 5. Security

5.1 The Processor shall implement the technical and organisational measures described in Annex 2, including encryption of Personal Data in transit and at rest.

## 6. Sub-processors

6.1 The Controller gives general authorisation for the Processor to engage Sub-processors, provided that each is bound by written terms no less protective than this Agreement.

## 7. Personal Data Breaches

7.1 The Processor shall notify the Controller of a Personal Data Breach within seven days of becoming aware of it.

7.2 The notice shall describe the breach, the categories of data affected and the measures taken.

## 9. Audits

9.1 The Processor shall make available to the Controller the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement.

9.3 The Controller's audit rights are satisfied by the Processor's most recent third-party certification report.

## 11. Return and deletion

11.1 At the end of the Services Agreement, the Processor shall delete all Personal Data within 60 days, unless the Controller asks for its return.

## 13. Governing law

13.1 This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
