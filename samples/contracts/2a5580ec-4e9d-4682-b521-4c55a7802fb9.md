# Master services agreement — Altamira Hotels S.L.

This Agreement is made on 1 October 2026 between Altamira Hotels S.L., a company registered in Spain (the "Customer"), and Tidewater Systems Ltd, a company registered in Ireland (the "Supplier").

The following Schedules are attached and form part of this Agreement: Schedule 1 (Services), Schedule 2 (Charges), Schedule 3 (Service Levels and Service Credits), Schedule 4 (Statement of Work template), Schedule 5 (Governance) and Schedule 6 (Exit).

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Services" means the IT services described in Schedule 1 and in each Statement of Work, including the hosting and support of the Customer's property management system, guest Wi-Fi and service desk for its 42 hotels. "Effective Date" means the date of this Agreement.

## 2. Scope

2.1 The Supplier shall provide the Services from the Effective Date in accordance with this Agreement.

2.3 The Supplier shall provide the Services and any other services reasonably necessary for the operation of the Customer's hotels.

## 3. Statements of Work

3.1 The Customer may vary any Statement of Work by written notice to the Supplier.

## 4. Term

4.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of five years (the "Initial Term"), and then from year to year until terminated.

## 5. Invoicing and payment

5.1 The Supplier shall invoice the Charges monthly in arrears.

5.4 The Customer shall pay each undisputed invoice within 90 days of receipt.

5.6 The Customer may set off against any sum due to the Supplier any amount owed by the Supplier under any contract.

## 6. Charges

6.1 The Charges are set out in Schedule 2 and are exclusive of VAT.

6.2 The Charges shall remain fixed for the Initial Term of five years.

## 7. Service levels

7.1 For each Service Level failure, the Supplier shall credit the amount set out in Schedule 3.

7.3 Service credits are without prejudice to any other right or remedy of the Customer.

7.5 The Service Levels shall apply from the Effective Date.

## 9. Acceptance

9.1 Deliverables shall be deemed accepted only when the Customer confirms in writing that they are satisfactory.

## 11. Intellectual property

11.2 All Intellectual Property Rights in any materials used or created in providing the Services shall vest in the Customer.

## 12. Liability

12.1 Nothing in this Agreement shall limit the Supplier's liability.

12.4 The Supplier shall indemnify the Customer against all claims by guests, employees or any third party connected with the Services.

## 13. Insurance

13.1 The Supplier shall maintain professional indemnity and cyber insurance of at least EUR 10,000,000 for each claim.

## 14. Data protection

14.1 Each party shall comply with the General Data Protection Regulation.

14.3 The Parties shall comply with the data processing terms set out in Schedule 7.

## 15. Subcontracting

15.1 The Supplier shall not subcontract any part of the Services without the Customer's prior written consent.

## 16. Termination

16.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within 30 days.

16.2 The Customer may terminate this Agreement at any time on 30 days' notice.

16.5 On termination, the Supplier shall provide all assistance required to transfer the Services to a replacement supplier.

## 17. Step-in

17.1 The Customer may take over the performance of any Service whenever it considers it necessary.

## 18. Personnel

18.1 The Supplier shall ensure that its personnel comply with the Customer's site rules when working at a hotel.

18.2 The Supplier shall not solicit or employ any employee of the Customer during the term and for 12 months after.

## 19. Benchmarking

19.1 If a benchmark shows the Charges exceed the market rate, the Supplier shall reduce them accordingly.

## 21. Force majeure

21.1 Neither party is liable for a failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

21.3 Force majeure shall not include any failure of the Supplier's systems, including as a result of cyber attacks.

## 24. Governing law and disputes

24.1 Any dispute shall be submitted exclusively to the courts of Madrid.

24.2 This Agreement is governed by Spanish law.
