# Share purchase agreement — Kestrel Microsystems Ltd

This Agreement is made on 27 October 2026 between the persons listed in Schedule 1, who sell together and each of whom is referred to as the "Seller", and Aldermoor Technologies plc (the "Purchaser").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Company" means Kestrel Microsystems Ltd. "Shares" means the entire issued share capital of the Company. "Completion" means completion of the sale of the Shares under clause 5.

## 2. Sale and purchase

2.1 The Seller shall sell, and the Purchaser shall buy, the Shares free from all encumbrances.

## 3. Price

3.1 The price for the Shares is £42,000,000 (the "Purchase Price"), plus any Earn-Out.

## 4. Earn-out

4.1 The Purchaser shall pay an additional amount of up to £8,000,000 (the "Earn-Out") if the Company reaches the EBITDA targets in Schedule 4.

4.5 The Earn-Out shall be calculated on the audited EBITDA of the Company for the financial year 2027.

## 5. Completion

5.1 Completion takes place on the date of this Agreement. At Completion, the Seller shall deliver the documents listed in Schedule 2.

## 6. Warranties

6.1 The Seller gives the warranties in Schedule 3 (the "Warranties") to the Purchaser at the date of this Agreement.

6.2 Each Warranty is given only so far as the Seller is aware, having made no enquiries.

## 7. Limitations

7.1 The Seller shall not be liable for any claim unless notified within 12 months of Completion.

7.3 No claim may be brought unless the aggregate amount of all claims exceeds 5% of the Purchase Price.

7.4 The Seller's total liability for all claims shall not exceed the Purchase Price.

## 8. Restrictive covenants

8.1 For three years after Completion, the Seller shall not carry on a business competing with the Company in the United Kingdom.

## 9. Payment

9.1 The Purchase Price shall be paid in full in cash at Completion.

## 10. Management

10.1 The Sellers who are directors of the Company shall remain as directors and managers until at least 31 December 2027.

## 15. Governing law

15.1 This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
