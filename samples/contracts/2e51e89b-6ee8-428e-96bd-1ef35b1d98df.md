# Shareholders' agreement — Quillon Labs B.V.

This Agreement is made on 16 March 2026 between the founders listed in Schedule 1 (the "Founders"), Rookwood Ventures Fund II LP ("Rookwood"), Ostler Capital Partners B.V. ("Ostler"), and Quillon Labs B.V. (the "Company"). Rookwood and Ostler are the "Investors".

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Shares" means the ordinary and preference shares in the capital of the Company. "Shareholders" means the holders of Shares from time to time.

## 4. The Board

4.1 The Board consists of up to five directors: two appointed by the Founders, one by Rookwood (the "Investor Director"), one by Ostler, and one independent director appointed by the Board.

## 5. Board decisions

5.1 All decisions of the Board shall be taken by a simple majority of the directors present.

5.2 A meeting of the Board is quorate when three directors are present.

## 7. Transfers of Shares

7.1 No Shareholder may transfer Shares except in accordance with this Agreement. Before any transfer, the other Shareholders have a right of first refusal pro rata to their holdings.

## 8. Tag-along and drag-along

8.1 If Shareholders holding more than 50% of the Shares agree to sell to a third party, every other Shareholder may require the buyer to buy its Shares on the same terms.

8.2 If Shareholders holding more than 50% of the Shares accept an offer, all other Shareholders shall sell their Shares on the same terms.

## 9. Anti-dilution

9.1 If the Company issues new Shares at a price per Share below the price paid by the Investors, the Investors are entitled to a broad-based weighted average adjustment.

## 11. Leavers

11.1 A Founder who stops being an employee or director of the Company is a "Leaver".

11.3 A Leaver shall transfer their Shares at Fair Value or at nominal value, as the Board determines.

## 13. Information rights

13.1 The Company shall send each Investor its monthly management accounts within 20 days of the end of each month and its annual budget before the start of each financial year.

## 20. Governing law

20.1 This Agreement is governed by Dutch law, and the courts of Amsterdam have exclusive jurisdiction.
