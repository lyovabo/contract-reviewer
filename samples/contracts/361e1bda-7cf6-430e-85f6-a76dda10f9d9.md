# Lease of part — Second floor, 27 Albion Quay, Bristol (revised draft)

This Lease is made on 14 October 2026 between Albion Quay Estates Ltd (the "Landlord") and Fernhill Analytics Ltd (the "Tenant"). This draft replaces the draft of 3 June 2026.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Building" means 27 Albion Quay, Bristol BS1, and "Premises" means the whole of the second floor of the Building, as shown edged red on Plan 1.

1.2 "Term" means ten years from and including 1 January 2027.

## 2. Grant

2.1 The Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for the Term, together with the rights set out in Schedule 1, including the use of the common entrance, lifts and stairs.

## 3. Rent

3.1 The Tenant shall pay the initial rent of £182,000 a year, plus VAT, by equal quarterly payments in advance on the usual quarter days.

## 4. Rent indexation

4.1 The rent shall be increased annually in line with the Consumer Price Index.

4.2 Each increase takes effect on the anniversary of the start of the Term and is calculated by reference to the index published for the month three months before that date.

## 5. Service charge

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a fair proportion, as reasonably determined by the Landlord, of the costs of running and maintaining the common parts of the Building, capped at £14 per square foot each year.

## 8. Use

8.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises only as offices within Class E(g)(i).

## 9. Repair

9.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises clean and tidy and shall decorate it in the last year of the Term.

9.2 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises in good repair. The Landlord shall keep the roof, foundations and main structure of the Building in good repair.

## 11. Alienation

11.1 The Tenant shall not underlet or share occupation of part only of the Premises.

11.4 The Tenant shall not assign this Lease without the Landlord's prior written consent, which may be withheld at its absolute discretion.

## 14. Insurance

14.1 The Landlord shall insure the Building against the usual risks, and the Tenant shall pay a fair proportion of the premium.

## 17. Termination

17.1 The Tenant may terminate this Lease on the fifth anniversary of the Term by giving at least six months' written notice, provided that it has paid all rent due.

17.2 The Landlord may terminate this Lease on six months' notice if it intends to redevelop the Building.

## 20. Governing law

20.1 This Lease is governed by the law of England and Wales.
