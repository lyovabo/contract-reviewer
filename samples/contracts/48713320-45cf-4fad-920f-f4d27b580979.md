# Distribution agreement — Brûlerie Montclair SAS

This Agreement is made on 2 April 2026 between Brûlerie Montclair SAS (the "Supplier") and Hollis & Wren Fine Foods Ltd (the "Distributor").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Products" means the Supplier's roasted coffee products listed in Schedule 1. "Territory" means the United Kingdom and Ireland.

## 3. Appointment

3.1 The Distributor is appointed exclusive distributor in the Territory, without prejudice to the Supplier's right to sell directly to customers.

3.2 The Distributor shall not actively seek customers for the Products outside the Territory.

## 4. Prices

4.1 The Distributor shall buy the Products at the Supplier's distributor price list in force on the date of each order, and is free to set its own resale prices.

## 6. Minimum purchases

6.1 The Distributor shall buy Products worth at least EUR 400,000 in each contract year (the "Minimum Annual Purchases").

6.2 If the Distributor fails to meet the Minimum Annual Purchases, the Supplier may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect.

## 9. Trade marks

9.1 The Distributor may use the Supplier's trade marks only to promote and sell the Products in the Territory.

## 17. Term

17.1 This Agreement runs for five years from its date and then continues until terminated by either party on six months' written notice.

## 19. Consequences of termination

19.1 Neither party shall be entitled to any compensation or indemnity upon expiry or termination of this Agreement.

19.2 On termination, the Distributor shall stop using the Supplier's trade marks.

## 21. Governing law

21.1 This Agreement is governed by French law, and the Commercial Court of Lyon has exclusive jurisdiction.
