# SaaS subscription agreement — Ledgerly Cloud Ltd

This Agreement is made on 7 July 2026 between Ledgerly Cloud Ltd (the "Supplier") and Harrowgate Retail Group plc (the "Customer").

## 1. The Service

1.1 The Supplier shall provide the Ledgerly accounts-payable platform described in the Order Form (the "Service") to the Customer's authorised users.

## 3. Term and renewal

3.1 The initial subscription term is 12 months from the date of the Order Form.

3.4 The subscription renews automatically for successive 12-month terms unless cancelled at least 90 days before renewal.

## 4. Fees

4.1 The Customer shall pay the annual subscription fee set out in the Order Form, invoiced monthly in advance, within 30 days of the invoice date.

## 5. Availability and support

5.1 The Supplier shall use reasonable efforts to make the Service available 99.5% of the time in each month, excluding planned maintenance notified at least 48 hours in advance.

## 6. Customer data

6.1 The Customer owns all data it uploads to the Service ("Customer Data"). The Supplier shall process Customer Data only to provide the Service and in accordance with the data processing addendum.

6.2 The Supplier shall back up Customer Data daily and keep backups for 30 days.

## 8. Liability

8.1 The Supplier's total liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the month preceding the claim.

8.2 Neither party is liable for loss of profits or for indirect or consequential loss.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each party shall keep the other's confidential information secret and use it only to perform this Agreement.

## 12. Changes to the Service

12.1 The Supplier may update the Service to fix defects or improve security.

12.3 The Supplier may modify the features of the Service from time to time without notice.

## 14. Termination

14.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement on written notice if the other commits a material breach that is not remedied within 30 days.

14.2 On termination, the Customer's right to use the Service ends immediately.

## 16. Governing law

16.1 This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
