# Intellectual property assignment — Kanso Studio

This Agreement is made on 18 August 2026 between Kanso Studio, a design studio registered in Portugal (the "Designer"), and Fieldfare Outdoor Ltd (the "Company").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Designs" means the brand identity work created by the Designer for the Company under the engagement letter of 3 May 2026, including logos, typography and packaging. "Final Designs" means the versions of the Designs approved by the Company in writing. "Fee" means EUR 48,000.

## 2. Assignment

2.1 The Designer assigns to the Company all rights in the Final Designs.

2.2 The assignment includes the right to register the Final Designs as trade marks or designs anywhere in the world.

## 3. Payment

3.1 The Company shall pay the Fee within 30 days of delivery of the Final Designs.

3.2 The assignment shall take effect only upon payment in full of the Fee.

## 4. Warranties

4.1 The Designer warrants that the Designs are its original work and do not infringe the rights of any third party.

## 5. Moral rights and credit

5.1 The Designer may show the Final Designs in its portfolio after the Company's public launch.

5.3 The Designer retains all moral rights in the Designs.

## 8. Governing law

8.1 This Agreement is governed by Portuguese law.
