# Non-disclosure agreement

This Agreement is made on 1 September 2026 between Halden Robotics AB, a company registered in Sweden ("Halden"), and Norrvik Automation Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (the "Recipient").

## 1. Purpose and definitions

1.1 Halden and the Recipient are discussing a possible joint development of warehouse picking robots (the "Purpose").

1.2 "Confidential Information" means all technical, commercial and financial information disclosed for the Purpose, in any form, that is marked as confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

## 2. Confidentiality

2.1 The Recipient shall keep confidential all Confidential Information disclosed by Halden Robotics AB.

2.2 The Recipient shall use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose, and shall disclose it only to those of its employees and advisers who need to know it for the Purpose and are bound by duties of confidentiality no less strict than these.

2.3 The Recipient shall protect the Confidential Information with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information, and never less than reasonable care.

## 3. Exceptions

3.1 Clause 2 does not apply to information that is or becomes public other than through a breach of this Agreement, that the Recipient already lawfully held, or that it develops independently.

3.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information when required by law or by a court, after giving Halden as much notice as the law allows.

## 4. Ownership

4.1 All Confidential Information remains the property of Halden. Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any licence or other right in it.

## 5. Term

5.1 This Agreement starts on the date above and continues for three years. The duties in clause 2 survive for five years after it ends.

## 6. General

6.1 This Agreement does not oblige either party to enter into any further agreement.

6.2 Halden may seek an injunction for any actual or threatened breach of clause 2, in addition to any other remedy.

6.3 Nothing in this Agreement restricts the use of information retained in the unaided memory of Halden's personnel.

6.4 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Sweden, and the courts of Stockholm have exclusive jurisdiction.
