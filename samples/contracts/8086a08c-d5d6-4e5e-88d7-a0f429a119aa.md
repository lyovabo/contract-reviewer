# Settlement agreement — Castellan Freight Ltd and Brenner Logistik GmbH

This Agreement is made on 4 November 2026 between Castellan Freight Ltd ("Castellan") and Brenner Logistik GmbH ("Brenner"), each a "Party".

## 1. Background

1.1 The Parties entered into a haulage contract dated 10 January 2025 (the "Haulage Contract"). A dispute has arisen about unpaid invoices and damaged consignments under it (the "Dispute").

## 2. Settlement payment

2.1 Brenner shall pay Castellan EUR 180,000 within 14 days of the date of this Agreement, in full and final settlement of the Dispute.

2.2 Each Party bears its own legal costs.

## 3. Release

3.1 Each Party releases the other from all claims, whether known or unknown, arising from any dealings between them.

## 4. Proceedings

4.1 Within seven days of receiving the settlement payment, Castellan shall withdraw the claim it has issued in the Commercial Court.

## 5. No admission

5.1 This Agreement is made without any admission of liability by either Party.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.2 The Parties shall not disclose the existence or terms of this Agreement to any person.

## 9. Governing law

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
