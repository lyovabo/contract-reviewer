# Supply agreement — Vortex Polymers GmbH

This Agreement is made on 12 May 2026 between Vortex Polymers GmbH (the "Supplier") and Calder Packaging Ltd (the "Buyer").

## 1. Supply

1.1 The Supplier shall supply the polymer granulates listed in Schedule 1 (the "Goods") in the quantities the Buyer orders under this Agreement.

## 3. Orders and forecasts

3.1 The Buyer shall give the Supplier a non-binding rolling six-month forecast each month. Orders become binding when the Supplier accepts them in writing.

## 5. Prices and payment

5.1 The prices for the Goods are set out in Schedule 2 and are ex works, excluding VAT.

5.2 The Supplier may adjust prices on 30 days' written notice to reflect changes in raw material costs.

5.3 The Buyer shall pay each invoice within 60 days of the end of the month in which it is received.

## 7. Delivery

7.1 Delivery dates are estimates only and time shall not be of the essence.

7.2 Risk in the Goods passes to the Buyer on delivery, and title passes on payment in full.

## 10. Quality

10.1 The Goods shall conform to the specifications in Schedule 1 and be free from defects in material and manufacture.

## 15. Defects

15.1 The Buyer shall inspect each delivery on arrival.

15.4 Any claim for defective goods must be notified within 14 days of delivery.

15.5 The Supplier's only obligation for defective goods is to replace them or refund their price, at its option.

## 18. Term and termination

18.1 This Agreement runs for three years and then continues until terminated by either party on six months' written notice.

## 22. Governing law

22.1 This Agreement is governed by German law, excluding the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods.
