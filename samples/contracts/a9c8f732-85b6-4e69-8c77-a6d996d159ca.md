# Sponsorship agreement — Velocity Cycling Club

This Agreement is made on 15 January 2026 between Velocity Cycling Club (the "Club") and Pembury Mutual Insurance Ltd (the "Sponsor").

## 1. Sponsorship

1.1 The Sponsor shall be the main sponsor of the Club's men's and women's road teams for the 2026, 2027 and 2028 seasons.

## 3. Sponsorship fee

3.1 The Sponsor shall pay a fee of £450,000 for each season, in two equal instalments on 1 February and 1 July.

## 4. Rights of the Sponsor

4.1 The Sponsor may use the Club's name, logo and images of its riders in its advertising, with the Club's approval, not to be unreasonably withheld.

4.2 The Club shall display the Sponsor's logo on its kit and materials.

## 6. Obligations of the Club

6.1 The Club shall enter its teams in at least ten UCI-registered races each season and make two riders available for four sponsor events each year.

## 9. Termination

9.1 The Sponsor may terminate this Agreement only if the Club becomes insolvent.

9.2 The Club may terminate this Agreement if the Sponsor fails to pay any instalment within 30 days of its due date.

## 12. Governing law

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
