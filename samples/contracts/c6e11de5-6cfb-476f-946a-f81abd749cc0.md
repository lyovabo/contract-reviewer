# Lease of part — Second floor, 27 Albion Quay, Bristol

This Lease is made on 3 June 2026 between Albion Quay Estates Ltd (the "Landlord") and Fernhill Analytics Ltd (the "Tenant").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Building" means 27 Albion Quay, Bristol BS1, and "Premises" means the whole of the second floor of the Building, as shown edged red on Plan 1.

1.2 "Term" means ten years from and including 1 July 2026.

## 2. Grant

2.1 The Landlord lets the Premises to the Tenant for the Term, together with the rights set out in Schedule 1, including the use of the common entrance, lifts and stairs.

## 3. Rent

3.1 The Tenant shall pay the initial rent of £186,000 a year, plus VAT, by equal quarterly payments in advance on the usual quarter days.

## 4. Rent review

4.1 The rent shall be reviewed every third year and shall not be less than the rent payable immediately before the review date.

4.2 The reviewed rent shall be the open market rent agreed between the parties or, failing agreement, determined by an independent surveyor.

## 5. Service charge

5.1 The Tenant shall pay a fair proportion, as reasonably determined by the Landlord, of the costs of running and maintaining the common parts of the Building.

## 8. Use

8.1 The Tenant shall use the Premises only as offices within Class E(g)(i).

## 9. Repair

9.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Premises clean and tidy and shall decorate it in the last year of the Term.

9.2 The Tenant shall keep the Premises, including the roof, foundations and main structure, in good and substantial repair.

## 11. Alienation

11.1 The Tenant shall not assign the whole of this Lease without the Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

11.2 The Tenant shall not underlet or share occupation of part only of the Premises.

## 14. Insurance

14.1 The Landlord shall insure the Building against the usual risks, and the Tenant shall pay a fair proportion of the premium.

## 17. Termination

17.1 The Tenant may terminate this Lease on the fifth anniversary of the Term by giving at least six months' written notice, provided that it has paid all rent due.

## 20. Governing law

20.1 This Lease is governed by the law of England and Wales.
