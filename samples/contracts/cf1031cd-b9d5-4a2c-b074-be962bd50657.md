# Employment contract — Head of Data Engineering

This contract is made on 20 August 2026 between Tessaly Analytics GmbH (the "Company") and Mara Lindqvist (the "Employee").

## 1. Start date and role

1.1 The Employee's employment starts on 1 October 2026. The Employee is employed as Head of Data Engineering and reports to the Chief Technology Officer.

## 2. Probation

2.1 The first six months are a probationary period, during which either party may end the employment on two weeks' notice.

## 5. Salary and benefits

5.1 The Company shall pay a gross salary of EUR 138,000 a year in twelve monthly instalments.

5.2 The Employee is eligible for the Company's annual bonus plan, at the Company's discretion.

## 6. Working time

6.1 Normal working hours are 40 hours a week, Monday to Friday, with flexible start and finish times.

6.2 The salary covers all hours worked, including any work outside normal working hours.

## 7. Holidays

7.1 The Employee is entitled to 28 working days' paid holiday a year, in addition to public holidays.

## 9. Intellectual property

9.1 The Employee shall promptly disclose to the Company any invention, design or software made in connection with the employment.

9.3 All inventions made by the Employee during the term of employment shall belong to the Company.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 The Employee shall keep the Company's confidential information secret during and after the employment.

## 12. Termination

12.1 After probation, either party may end the employment on three months' written notice to the end of a calendar month.

## 14. Restrictions after termination

14.1 For 24 months after termination, the Employee shall not work for any business that competes with the Company anywhere in Europe.

14.2 For 12 months after termination, the Employee shall not solicit any employee of the Company to leave.

## 16. Governing law

16.1 This contract is governed by German law.
