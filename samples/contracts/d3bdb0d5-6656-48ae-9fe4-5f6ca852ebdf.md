# Construction contract — Riverside Library extension, Ghent

This Contract is made on 11 May 2026 between Leiedal Cultural Trust (the "Employer") and Baertsoen Bouw NV (the "Contractor").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Works" means the design and construction of the two-storey extension to the Riverside Library, Ghent, as described in the Employer's Requirements. "Contract Sum" means EUR 7,800,000, excluding VAT.

## 2. The Works

2.1 The Contractor shall carry out and complete the Works in a proper and workmanlike manner, in accordance with this Contract and all applicable laws.

## 8. Time

8.1 The Contractor shall start the Works on 1 July 2026 and achieve Practical Completion by 30 September 2027 (the "Completion Date").

8.4 The Contractor shall be entitled to an extension of time for any delay to the Works not caused by the Contractor.

## 12. Liquidated damages

12.1 Liquidated damages for delay shall not exceed 2% of the Contract Sum.

12.2 Subject to clause 12.1, the Contractor shall pay liquidated damages of EUR 6,000 for each week by which Practical Completion is later than the Completion Date.

## 14. Payment

14.1 The Contractor shall apply for payment monthly. The Employer shall pay the amount certified by the Employer's architect within 30 days.

## 15. Retention

15.1 The Employer may retain 5% of each certified amount (the "Retention").

15.2 The Retention shall be released in full upon Practical Completion.

## 17. Defects

17.1 The Contractor shall make good any defects notified within 12 months after Practical Completion (the "Defects Period").

## 20. Design

20.1 The Contractor shall complete the design of the Works in accordance with the Employer's Requirements.

20.2 The design prepared before the date of this Contract by the Employer's architect, Studio Vandevelde, is included in the Employer's Requirements.

## 23. Insurance

23.1 The Contractor shall take out all-risks insurance for the Works in the joint names of the Employer and the Contractor.

## 28. Governing law

28.1 This Contract is governed by Belgian law, and the courts of Ghent have exclusive jurisdiction.
