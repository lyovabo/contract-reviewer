# Term loan facility agreement — Meridian Credit Bank S.A.

This Agreement is made on 30 June 2026 between Meridian Credit Bank S.A. (the "Lender") and Ostrava Components a.s. (the "Borrower").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Group" means the Borrower and its subsidiaries. "Financial Indebtedness" means any indebtedness for borrowed money, finance leases and guarantees. "Leverage" means the ratio of total net debt of the Group to consolidated EBITDA for the preceding 12 months.

## 2. The facility

2.1 The Lender makes available a term loan of EUR 25,000,000 to refinance existing debt and for general corporate purposes.

## 7. Repayment

7.1 The Borrower shall repay the loan in equal semi-annual instalments, with the final instalment due on the fifth anniversary of this Agreement.

## 8. Interest

8.1 Interest accrues at EURIBOR plus a margin of 2.75% a year and is payable at the end of each three-month interest period.

## 9. Prepayment

9.1 The Borrower may prepay all or part of the loan on 10 business days' notice, in a minimum amount of EUR 1,000,000.

9.4 Any voluntary prepayment shall be subject to a prepayment fee of 2% of the amount prepaid.

## 17. Information undertakings

17.1 The Borrower shall deliver its audited consolidated accounts within 120 days of each financial year end, and monthly management accounts within 30 days of each month end.

## 18. Financial covenants

18.1 The Borrower shall ensure that interest cover is at least 4.0:1.

18.3 The Borrower shall ensure that Leverage does not exceed 3.0:1, tested on the last day of each month.

## 21. Events of default

21.1 Each of the events in this clause 21.1 is an Event of Default:

(a) the Borrower fails to pay any amount due under this Agreement within three business days of its due date;

(b) the Borrower breaches clause 18;

(f) An Event of Default occurs if any Financial Indebtedness of any member of the Group is not paid when due.

21.2 At any time after an Event of Default, the Lender may cancel the facility and declare the loan immediately due and payable.

## 30. Governing law

30.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Luxembourg.
